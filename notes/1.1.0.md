- **Notes on a place in a document.** Write a note about a file, a page or the exact words you selected, and it remembers the words that were there.
- **Narrows as you do.** With a document open you see all its notes; on a page, that page's notes; with a passage selected, the notes on it, and a count of the rest.
- **Notes follow the text.** When the document changes, a note stays with the words it was written about.
- **Kept in your project.** Notes are saved inside the project they are about, and agents can read and add them too.
