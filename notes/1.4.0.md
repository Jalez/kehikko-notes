- **Search the notes with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in Notes to open Kehikot's find bar: every match in the notes and their quotes is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), the list scrolls to the current one, Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar.
- **Your place in the list is kept.** Each Notes container remembers how far down the list you had scrolled, and comes back there when you return to the kehikko or reopen the app.
- **No flash while it opens.** Kehikot keeps its cover up until the notes are drawn, so you no longer see an empty list first.
- **A page that fails says so.** If the notes cannot be drawn, Kehikot is told what went wrong instead of showing a blank container.
